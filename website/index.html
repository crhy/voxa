Designed around a simple idea

The assistant should live on your machine.

01

Speak naturally

Voice is the primary interface. Voxa listens, transcribes, responds, and keeps the interaction focused on the assistant—not a chat box.

02

Run local models

Choose local AI through Ollama or llama.cpp. Keep everyday assistant work close to the machine and under your control.

03

See what it's doing

A task-oriented interface surfaces active work, state, model selection, choices, attachments, and explicit ACTIVE/OFFLINE control.

04

Grow into actions

Voxa is evolving from conversation into a structured desktop agent with bounded skills, permissions, confirmations, and auditable tasks.
