# Voxa 0.1.9 rc6 — local Flatpak (not published)

Local test bundle for 0.1.9, built from branch `strata/v11-voxa-live-bargein-hearing-latency`
(issue #72) and **not** pushed to Flathub. It is rc5 plus the v11 hearing fixes and the v10
scripted-listening merge. It never auto-updates and never overwrites an earlier local rc.

**Status: a work in progress.** Steps 3-6 are implemented, committed and confirmed on the
desktop; step 2 (the reference tap reading non-silent on the real flatpak sink) is **not yet
met** - the honest live numbers below show the reference still goes silent on some replies.

## What is in it

Everything in rc5 (`docs/release/0.1.9-rc5-local.md`) plus, from issue #72 task v11:

- **v10 merged in (`start-listening` / `stop-listening` app actions + VoxaTest preflight).**
  `gapplication action io.github.crhy.voxa start-listening` puts her in Active for live tests.
- **A degraded reference never abstains forever (`voxa/bargectl.py`).** When attribution can
  only abstain because the reference is unusable (silent tap while she speaks, or an unhealthy
  alignment), the controller now promotes by the turn-wide text veto: heard words that are not
  in her turn (>= 3 new words, or the wake word + >= 2) interrupt her. Her own words still never
  interrupt. **Confirmed live:** a `degraded reference: turn-wide text veto promoted to user`
  line produced an `INTERRUPT` in the desktop run.
- **A held capture span never crosses a turn and is capped at 15 s (`voxa/capture.py`,
  `voxa/conversation.py`, `voxa/window.py`).** `MAX_PINNED_SECONDS` 120 -> 15; `close_span()`
  drops the pin and clears the candidate transcript at every turn boundary. **Confirmed live:**
  `promote ... span_seconds=14.996` (the 15 s cap) where the v09 log grew one span past 60 s.
- **Barge.log logs each turn's stages with ms (`voxa/window.py`).** `user-end`, `stt-done`,
  `request heard` and `speak-start` now carry `turn_ms`, and the timestamp has milliseconds.
- **VoxaTest `--talk-over` and a 1.0 s settle (`voxatest/`).** `--talk-over` speaks the next
  question 3 s into a reply longer than ~4 s so her stop latency is measured; `settle_seconds`
  1.5 -> 1.0.

## Live measurements (desktop, DISPLAY :0.0, this rc6 bundle installed)

Driving the installed bundle with `start-listening` + `read-file` (three sentences):

- **reference silent while speaking: 1 of 2 checks** — NOT yet 0. The whole-reply (read-file)
  reply is no longer silent (attribution reports `alignment unhealthy`, i.e. the reference is
  active), but a `piece`-path reply still falls back to
  `reference active by definition while speaking: silent tap, abstain`. **Step 2 is not done.**
  The per-reply `render-tap` diagnostic (peak/samples) added in
  `voxa/window.py:_note_reply_end` does not yet fire on the conversation reply path, so the
  attach/error/peak numbers are not visible; the real-sink tap root cause is open.
- interrupts obeyed: degraded-reference promote -> `INTERRUPT` observed live (step 3 works live).
- held span: capped at 15 s live (`span_seconds=14.996`), never across turns (step 4 works live).
- turn latency (`barge.log` `turn_ms`): `stt-done turn_ms=552`, `request heard turn_ms=1`, but
  `speak-start turn_ms=15240` - the big stage is the model generating the reply (~15 s), not
  endpoint/transcription (step 5 measured; the <= 3 s target is dominated by generation).

## Not yet done (why the gate fails step 2)

  peak is measurable.
- VoxaTest 5 talk-over cases and stop-latency p50/p95 have not been run live yet.
