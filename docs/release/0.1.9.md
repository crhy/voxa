# Voxa 0.1.9 — release candidate (2026-11-23)

Every fleet branch that passed its gate since 0.1.8 (2026-10-26) is merged here, plus the
hearing chain ending at strata/f183-voxa-hear-t9-probe-withhold-last-cut (f163..f181 plus f183).
One line each, in user language: what a Voxa user can now do.

## Hearing and talking

- She learns her hearing per speaker and microphone: the saved filter for the pair you talk
  through is her starting point, and what she learns during use is saved back under it. (strata/f017-voxa-hearing-profile-live, #72)
- "Which gpu is voxa using" / "put voxa on the other gpu" style hearing status and the exact
  echo delay are measured and reported; the clip renderer, pipeline runner, metrics report and
  baseline ratchet for the hearing roadmap are in the tree. (strata/f124-voxa-hear-b1-clip-renderer, strata/f125-voxa-hear-b2-pipeline-runner, strata/f126-voxa-hear-b3-metrics-report, strata/f127-voxa-hear-b5-baseline-ratchet, #72)
- Test references are rendered from the exact TTS audio, and the echo delay keeps tracking
  through drift between the microphone and speaker clocks. (strata/f128-voxa-hear-t1-exact-tts-reference, strata/f130-voxa-hear-t3-delay-drift, #72)
- VoxaTest scores what Voxa heard against what was said, runs offline (no speakers, no
  microphone), and keeps its soak runs from tripping over each other. (strata/f142-voxa-hear-v1-voxatest-comprehension, strata/f143-voxa-hear-v2-voxatest-offline-audio, strata/f145-voxa-hear-v4-voxatest-soak-isolation, #72)
- With her microphone paused she no longer hears things that are not there: no false triggers
  and no wake-word misses in the paused suite. (strata/f161-voxa-pause-no-false-positives, #136)
- The default echo cancellation is now the DTLN-aec neural echo stage: a small on-device model
  (its ONNX weights ship in the app) removes the computer's own sound before she listens,
  falling back to Voxa's own canceller on a machine without onnxruntime or the weights.
  (strata/h04-voxa-hearing2-ship-dtln-echo, #72)

## Hearing chain (f163..f183, #72)

The hearing roadmap's shipped slice. One line per task with its one-line summary.

- f163: the exact aligner measures the RECENT window only and snaps steps as two-hypothesis
  epochs; a confident pre-step delay never mis-leads a step, and the step evidence is the
  recent-window GCC, not a correlation across pair clips. (strata/f163-voxa-hear-full-truth-reanchor)
- f164: every linear arm (voxa/aec3/speex) is handed Voxa's locked render-timeline delay, and
  the locked-delay frontier rows are appended (speex 2.14→7.00 dB near-end safe, aec3
  31.43→5.17 dB but 24.96→12.12 dB user, voxa delay_err 9.29→0.07 ms). (strata/f164-voxa-hear-locked-delay-arms)
- f169: the residual echo stage rides on the exact-delay output, with ReferenceMask.set_delay
  (reference read at the locked delay, own lag estimate off), LockedResidualMask for the
  protect_db 6.0 rule, and the bench voxa-residual arm that hands the lock to the mask frame by
  frame. (strata/f169-voxa-hear-residual-on-lock)
- f171: the residual stage ships on the live capture path — exception-safe (the mask degrades
  alone, never the recording), set_delay only on frames the lock proved, and the bench voxa mode
  mirrors shipping (full suite leak 0.1928→0.1316, smoke ERLE 15.24→20.92, leak 0.1111→0.0992).
  (strata/f171-voxa-hear-ship-residual-lock)
- f173: the joint double-talk verdicts are wired into the LIVE hearing path — the capture thread
  parks one cleaned frame per window for the barge-in joint gate, the hearing worker drains the
  ring, runs the cleaned-stream Silero and the gate, and hands the joint verdicts back to the
  window; no model runs on the capture thread. (strata/f173-voxa-hear-t5-ship-joint-gate-live)
- f174: the task-8 turn states are wired in behind f173's joint suspect — reversible 12 dB
  own-stream duck on the joint suspect, hard-stop+assistant-context truncation on confirm,
  resume-at-word-boundary on reject; single decision source in bargectl with the pure turngate
  seam and ConversationHistory.truncate_last_assistant. (strata/f174-voxa-hear-t8-ship-turn-states)
- f177: a barge-in turn pre-rolls from the suspect onset on the CLEANED (post-AEC) stream, not
  the raw mic; Smart Turn v3.1 is parked and merged onto the shipped chain. (strata/f177-voxa-hear-t6-postaec-preroll)
- f178: the endpoint ARBITRATOR gates is_complete on the shipped joint suspect, not the Smart
  Turn probability alone. (strata/f178-voxa-hear-t9-endpoint-arbitrator)
- f179: device, headphone and Bluetooth changes are EXPLICIT epochs — the reference history, the
  canceller and the latency measurement all restart together (never per sentence); the chain
  plumbings the f137 device/epoch merge needs are tested. (strata/f179-voxa-hear-t10-ship-device-epochs)
- f180: ship the three REAL FIXES of parked f146 — the null-tool mislabel, the bench/live
  vocabulary-hint parity, and the two surviving ASR spelling fixes; the recogniser-bound 0.98
  hearing cap and the separate echo-defect lines stay out of the gate.
  (strata/f180-voxa-hear-v5-ship-routing-fixes)
- f181: the promote path waits the open joint suspect, the turn state and the still-arriving
  speech out — CaptureRing.promote_wait gates promote_pinned (force=True for accepted decisions
  and the hard cap), the runner holds the turn-CLOSURE events of a completion whose never-completed
  pinned span is still owed, and window retries a held HOLD handover at the evidence close.
  (strata/f181-voxa-hear-t89-promote-wait-joint-suspect)
- f183: the endpoint ARBITRATOR (probe side) withholds the last premature cut — VoiceDetector
  exposes the Silero HYSTERESIS state, runner._more_speech_arriving and window._hearing_evidence
  feed it to arbitrate_endpoint, so a complete=True from the pure-model/degraded path is withheld
  while gated speech is still arriving at the probe's own gate. Floor premature_cutoff_rate≤0.001.
  (strata/f183-voxa-hear-t9-probe-withhold-last-cut)

## Browser and web

- "Download the annual report" clicks the link you name and keeps the file in your
  Downloads/Voxa folder, saying what arrived and where. (strata/f028-voxa-browser-downloads, #40)
- A flight, hotel or price search reads the top three results back aloud instead of only
  opening the page. (strata/f078-voxa-read-search-results-back, #48)
- A product search reads the top three products aloud with price and rating. (strata/f114-voxa-read-product-results, #51)
- Web forms fill themselves from your saved profile. (strata/f111-voxa-fill-web-form-from-profile, #63)
- "Book a table for seven at quarter past eight" opens the restaurant's booking page filled
  in as far as it can. (strata/f115-voxa-open-restaurant-booking-page, #47)
- Paste a confirmation email and say "Track this package": she finds the tracking numbers,
  reads each carrier page, and keeps an order list you can ask about. (strata/f031-voxa-orders-from-pasted-text, #52)
