# Voxa 0.1.9 — release candidate (2026-11-21)

Every fleet branch that passed its gate since 0.1.8 (2026-10-26) is merged here.
One line each, in user language: what a Voxa user can now do.

## Hearing and talking

- She learns her hearing per speaker and microphone: the saved filter for the pair you talk
  through is her starting point, and what she learns during use is saved back under it. (strata/f017-voxa-hearing-profile-live, #72)
- "Which gpu is voxa using" / "put voxa on the other gpu" style hearing status and the exact
  echo delay are measured and reported; the clip renderer, pipeline runner, metrics report and
  baseline ratchet for the hearing roadmap are in the tree. (strata/f124-voxa-hear-b1-clip-renderer, strata/f125-voxa-hear-b2-pipeline-runner, strata/f126-voxa-hear-b3-metrics-report, strata/f127-voxa-hear-b5-baseline-ratchet, #72)
- Test references are rendered from the exact TTS audio, and the echo delay keeps tracking
  through drift between the microphone and speaker clocks. (strata/f128-voxa-hear-t1-exact-tts-reference, strata/f130-voxa-hear-t3-delay-drift, #72)
- VoxaTest scores what Voxa heard against what was said, runs offline (no speakers, no
  microphone), and keeps its soak runs from tripping over each other. (strata/f142-voxa-hear-v1-voxatest-comprehension, strata/f143-voxa-hear-v2-voxatest-offline-audio, strata/f145-voxa-hear-v4-voxatest-soak-isolation, #72)
- With her microphone paused she no longer hears things that are not there: no false triggers
  and no wake-word misses in the paused suite. (strata/f161-voxa-pause-no-false-positives, #136)

## Browser and web

- "Download the annual report" clicks the link you name and keeps the file in your
  Downloads/Voxa folder, saying what arrived and where. (strata/f028-voxa-browser-downloads, #40)
- A flight, hotel or price search reads the top three results back aloud instead of only
  opening the page. (strata/f078-voxa-read-search-results-back, #48)
- A product search reads the top three products aloud with price and rating. (strata/f114-voxa-read-product-results, #51)
- Web forms fill themselves from your saved profile. (strata/f111-voxa-fill-web-form-from-profile, #63)
- "Book a table for seven at quarter past eight" opens the restaurant's booking page filled
  in as far as it can. (strata/f115-voxa-open-restaurant-booking-page, #47)
- Paste a confirmation email and say "Track this package": she finds the tracking numbers,
  reads each carrier page, and keeps an order list you can ask about. (strata/f031-voxa-orders-from-pasted-text, #52)

## Home and money

- Recurring charges are noticed in your spending log: "what bills are due this week",
  "what subscriptions do I pay for", and bill reminders that fire the day they are due. (strata/f015-voxa-bills-and-subscriptions, #60)
- "Log this receipt" reads a receipt photo or scan, takes the TOTAL, works out the category
  and files it in the spending ledger; "undo the last expense" takes it back. (strata/f065-voxa-receipt-into-ledger, #62)
- Your Bitcoin balance and its current price, from a read-only exchange key. (strata/f077-voxa-bitcoin-price-and-balance, #46)
- Smart-home covers and locks, and scene colour, by voice. (strata/f069-voxa-home-covers-locks-colour, #44)
- The morning briefing now adds the bills that are coming due. (strata/f085-voxa-briefing-adds-bills, #57)

## Calendar and email

- "Move my dentist to Thursday at 4" moves the appointment and its reminder, and "when am I
  free tomorrow" names the first gap long enough. (strata/f044-voxa-calendar-move-and-free-time, #55)
- Routines run themselves on a weekday and time: "schedule the movie time routine for Friday
  at seven"; "unschedule" takes it off. (strata/f046-voxa-routine-schedules, #67)
- Voxa reads your own mailbox hands-free over IMAP and answers questions from it. (strata/f075-voxa-email-read-from-imap, #56)
- Meeting recordings become notes, filed to the archive, and "what was said at the meeting"
  reads them back. (strata/f073-voxa-meeting-notes-archive, #65)

## Apps and screen

- "Read this to me", "what's here", "summarize this" — she reads the window in front of you. (strata/f037-voxa-screen-context, #17)
- "Search my documents for lease" finds files by the words inside them, never touching them. (strata/f039-voxa-document-content-search, #64)
- A whole toolset for pressing real buttons in other applications. (strata/f079-voxa-app-control-toolset, #135)
- Interpreter mode: Voxa listens to a stream of your speech and runs each finished sentence. (strata/f060-voxa-interpreter-mode, #66)
- "Make me an invoice for two office chairs to Acme, net 40" writes the invoice as a document
  and opens it in LibreOffice. (strata/f014-voxa-invoice-from-one-sentence, #54)
- Secrets live in the system keyring and your name, address and phone number in your profile;
  both by voice. (strata/f010-voxa-secrets-profile, #41)
- "Download the latest Spaced ISO" checks the checksum before it is trusted, and torrent
  downloads verify first, download second. (strata/f042-voxa-download-spaced-iso, strata/f104-voxa-torrent-checksum-first-download, #131, #117)
- Background review keeps a turn log so she can say what she was thinking about. (strata/f095-voxa-background-review-turn-log, #116)

## Models and GPUs

- Ollama's model list works with no network: the catalogue is local. (strata/f019-voxa-ollama-models-when-offline, #27)
- The Ollama installer is hashed and checked before it is ever run with your password. (strata/f021-voxa-verify-ollama-script-before-root, #132)
- Preferences has a "Graphics card" row and the Local AI indicator says which card is in use. (strata/f024-voxa-gpu-choice-plumbing, #9)
- llama.cpp suggests brains sized for the machine, with "Fits this machine" / "Too big for
  this machine" marked on each rung. (strata/f056-voxa-llamacpp-brain-suggestions, #130)
- She names every graphics card she has and can take one of them: her small brain can sit on a
  different card from the big model's work. (strata/f063-voxa-second-gpu-brain, #113)

## Install and Flatpak

- The Flatpak drops the debug-symbol payload. (strata/f106-voxa-drop-debug-symbols-flatpak, #75)
- Panels and menus draw the two-tone symbolic icon, following the desktop theme's colours. (strata/f109-voxa-symbolic-menu-icon, #23)

## Tests

Offline only — no speakers, no microphone (`--offline-audio`). Live runs with speakers and
microphone happen later with rhY.

- Python suite: 4101 passed, 54 skipped (`python3 -m pytest -q tests`).
- `voxatest doctor --dry-run`: OK with 1 warning (no output sink on this machine, so the
  saved hearing profile cannot be found).
- Smoke suite (`test_cases_smoke.json`): 19/20; `hearing_word_accuracy = 0.9929` (gate
  threshold 0.9). The one failure is a memory-keeping case heard but not answered offline.
- Paused suite (`test_cases_paused.json`, issue #136): 87/87; `paused_false_trigger_rate =
  0.0000` and `paused_wake_recall = 1.0000` (both gate thresholds met: no false triggers,
  every wake word recalled).
- Full suite (`test_cases.json`): 33/36 — media-02, files-04-p1 and help-04 heard wrong by
  the offline re-hearing; pre-existing, unchanged by the merges.
- Extra suites: `test_cases_2` 39/44, `test_cases_3` 44/48, `test_cases_4` 3/4,
  `test_cases_features` 12/13 — same offline re-hearing misses as above.
- Capability suite (`test_cases_cap.json`): 383/458 — the capability checklist runs offline
  without replies spoken, so reply-grading cases fail by design in this mode.
- Hearing suite (`test_cases_hearing.json`): 16/68 — this is the suite from the parked
  hearing work (f144's gate failed with self_interrupt_count = 2); it is kept in the tree as
  a scoreboard, not as a gate.
