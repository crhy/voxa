`qwen3.5-0.8b-q4km:latest` (config before: `config-before-0826.json` (ollama_model
BF16-after-exit), after: exact tag - survived restart, verified in her log and `api/ps`:
724 MB qwen35 loaded). Warm: `ollama-warm2.json`, p50 latency above.

`reasons='reference silent: no echo possible'` lines and `speak-start path='piece'` - on the

settled=True ratio=5.260`) is her own playback. This is the playback-reference failure rhY

notes already spell out that suspect/confirm/reject text-decides-while-speaking design.
